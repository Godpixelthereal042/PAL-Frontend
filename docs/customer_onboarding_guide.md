# PAL Customer Onboarding Guide

Welcome to **PAL Executive Operating System**. This guide walks you through your first 15 minutes with PAL.

---

## Onboarding Walkthrough

### Step 1: Sign Up & Create Your Workspace
Navigate to `https://app.pal.ai/onboarding`. Enter your name, company email, and organization name. Your multi-tenant workspace will be provisioned automatically.

### Step 2: Invite Your Leadership Team
Go to **Settings → Team** and invite your executive team (CTO, COO, CFO) with appropriate RBAC roles:
- **Owner / CEO:** Full approval & action execution rights.
- **Admin:** System configuration & workspace settings.
- **Member:** Active project execution & task management.
- **Viewer:** Read-only access to dashboards & reports.

### Step 3: Connect Business Tools
Navigate to **Connectors** and authorize your core business tools:
- 💳 **Stripe:** Ingests billing ledger, invoices, and active subscriptions.
- 📅 **Google Workspace:** Ingests executive calendar and email alerts.
- 💬 **Slack:** Ingests team communication and notification alerts.
- 🐙 **GitHub:** Ingests engineering repositories and pull requests.

### Step 4: Review Your Business Brain
Navigate to **Business Brain**. PAL analyzes your connected tools and creates a living profile of your business offer, target customer segments, and primary KPIs.

### Step 5: Approve Your First AI Action Card
Look at the **Approval Queue** in your Executive Cockpit. Review PAL's first revenue recovery card, inspect the supporting evidence, and click **Approve Action**.
