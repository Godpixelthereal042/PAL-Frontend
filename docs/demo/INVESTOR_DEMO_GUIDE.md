# PAL Acme SaaS Investor Demo Walkthrough Guide (v3.0.0)

---

## Interactive Demo Flow: "Acme SaaS"

1. **Step 1: Switch to Investor Demo Mode**
   - Click the **"Investor Demo"** tab in the top CEO Navigation bar.

2. **Step 2: Morning Executive Briefing**
   > *"Good morning. Revenue risk increased due to enterprise pipeline slowdown. I discovered: **$18,400 monthly recovery opportunity**, **3 inefficient workflows**, and **2 expansion opportunities**. I prepared actions for approval."*

3. **Step 3: Evidence Inspection**
   - Click **"Inspect Evidence"** to review:
     - Stripe churn signals (4 enterprise accounts flagged inactive).
     - HubSpot pipeline latency (14-day delay in contract signature).
     - SaaS compute spend anomaly ($4,200 unutilized capacity).

4. **Step 4: One-Click Approval & Autonomous Execution**
   - Click **"Approve Execution"**.
   - PAL dispatches AI Customer Success Manager & Finance Controller agents to consolidate compute tiers and initiate renewal outreach.

5. **Step 5: Outcome Verification**
   - **Result**: $\$18,400$ monthly recovery secured, zero churned enterprise accounts, $18.5\times$ ROI multiple.
