## 3. Customer Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Will PAL make decisions without my permission?
**A:** No. PAL operates on a **Progressive Trust Architecture**. At Level 1 & 2 (default), every high-impact action generates an Approval Card requiring explicit CEO/Admin sign-off. As PAL demonstrates high decision accuracy (95%+), you may opt to grant autonomous execution privileges for specific categories.

### Q: How long does onboarding take?
**A:** Under 5 minutes. You sign up, connect your tools via OAuth, answer 4 questions to generate your Business Brain, and immediately receive your Day-0 First Value Moment.

## 4. Security & Compliance FAQ

### Q: Is my business data separated from other companies?
**A:** Yes. PAL enforces **Strict Multi-Tenant Database Isolation**. Every query is scoped to your unique `workspace_id`. Cross-workspace data leakage is mathematically impossible.

### Q: How is my authentication secured?
**A:** Passwords are encrypted using OWASP-compliant **Argon2id** (64MB memory cost). Session cookies use `SameSite: Lax`, `HttpOnly`, and `Secure` attributes with double-submit CSRF token validation.
